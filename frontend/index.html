How it works
01
Generate
Smart meters track solar energy production. Raw kWh data is signed and uploaded to IPFS with device attestation.
02
Verify & mint
Each credit is minted on Polygon against the IPFS data hash. CO₂ offset calculated at CEA's 0.82 kg/kWh grid emission factor.
03
Trade
Installers list verified credits on the marketplace at ₹415/credit. Buyers purchase to offset emissions — ownership transfers on-chain.
